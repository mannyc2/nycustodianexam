## Overlay legend

- `Z1` to `Z3`: neutral ordered zones;
- `T1`: draft target region;
- `D1`: draft decoy region;
- solid heavy polygon: target;
- dashed polygon: decoy;
- dashed broad boundaries: zones.

The polygons are draft authoring aids, not final scoring tolerance. Human marker-distribution testing remains pending.
