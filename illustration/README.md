# Illustration production corpus

This directory contains the maintained visual-production authority, supporting
historical pipelines, and non-production prototypes.

## Current authority

[`VISUAL_AUTHORING_POLICY.md`](VISUAL_AUTHORING_POLICY.md) controls production for
isolated tools, confusable comparisons, and hazard scenes.

The production route is Codex-native image generation. Accepted raster bytes are
the visual source of truth and ordinary immutable content inputs. Production
tools are not required to become SVG, CAD, or 3D assets.

`../docs/TAXONOMY.md` controls the admitted concepts and decisive distinctions.
All Tier A/B concepts remain launch scope.

## Public sample artwork

Publicly released official sample artwork may be supplied to Codex to copy its
high-level visual style: monochrome line quality, contour weight, sparse detail,
restrained hatching, whitespace, framing, and test-booklet economy.

It may not supply item content or a composition to reproduce. Never use secure,
remembered, candidate-recalled, or rights-unreviewed FOIL material. Production
subjects and compositions must be original and independently supported, and the
site must not imply official affiliation.

## Supporting and historical material

- [`TOOL_GEOMETRY_PIPELINE.md`](TOOL_GEOMETRY_PIPELINE.md) preserves the retired
  deterministic tool route as optional research/reference material.
- [`PIPELINE_SPEC.md`](PIPELINE_SPEC.md) preserves useful QA, accessibility,
  lineage, and prompt guidance from the recovered image-first pipeline; current
  policy overrides its old SVG and sample-reference restrictions.
- `examples/` contains historical SVG prototypes, not production assets.
- `../research/v2/tool-geometry-audit/` verifies the four recovered CAD/SVG POCs
  and records their retirement as production-art candidates.
- `../research/v2/hazard-scene-production/` preserves R2.9 contracts and its
  superseded deterministic-first recommendation. Its semantic, hotspot,
  accessibility, QA, and versioning contracts remain useful where they do not
  conflict with current policy.

## Release boundary

Every production image still requires independent content/mechanical-
plausibility, taxonomy, rights/similarity, accessibility, security, and
phone/print review at exact accepted hashes. Generation does not make an image
correct, and reproducible delivery does not require regenerating model output.
