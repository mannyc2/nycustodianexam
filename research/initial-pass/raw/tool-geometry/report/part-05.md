# 40. Honest limitations

This research did not:

- purchase or reproduce complete copyrighted standards;
- establish a universal license for supplier CAD;
- prove the absence of every possible open model;
- certify any POC geometry;
- conduct independent physical measurement of the four POC tools;
- obtain final mechanical SME approval;
- establish production-browser performance for GLB viewers;
- create or modify repository code;
- approve a storage/release strategy for binaries;
- research hazard-scene production to the same depth as isolated tools.

The POC is evidence that a deterministic pipeline can work. It is not evidence that the project can safely skip evidence gathering, measurement, rights review, or human review.
