## Accepted historical conclusions

- Standards and classification systems can support terminology, dimensions,
  constraints, and review; they do not automatically supply complete generic
  geometry or redistribution rights.
- Supplier and community geometry remains reference-only unless exact reuse and
  redistribution rights and taxonomy-specific mechanical suitability are
  established.
- Fixed, versioned scored views avoid changing the construct by exposing hidden
  decisive geometry. Phone, print, accessibility, rights, and answer-leak
  review remain required regardless of authoring method.
- The recovered CadQuery/OCCT pipeline reproduced 79 of 79 historical outputs
  twice. Adjustable- and pipe-wrench prototypes were reproducible and
  reviewable; cup- and flange-plunger prototypes required model/view rework.
- Reproducibility did not establish generic mechanical correctness. All four
  prototypes are accepted as closed pipeline evidence and retired as
  production-art candidates.

## Production disposition

The earlier deterministic-first recommendation is superseded. Production uses
reviewed Codex-native raster bytes under the visual policy; it does not require
STEP, GLB, SVG, CAD repair, or model regeneration. The released 65 tools and 14
comparisons have independent identity, lineage, content/mechanical, rights,
accessibility, leak, phone, and print review records.

No unresolved geometry-research question controls the current production route.
Per-asset specialist and scored-use gates remain attached to maintained content
records and must not be inferred from this historical synthesis.
