# SEO Strategy — NY Custodian Exam Study Site

**Research date:** 2026-08-18  
**Project:** Free study site for the NYS Entry-Level Custodians & Janitors written-test series  
**Baseline:** Canonical `LANDSCAPE.md`, `FACTBASE.md`, `OPEN.md`, `SCOPE.md`, and `TAXONOMY.md` from the project research corpus  
**Labels:** Confirmed / Corroborated / Inferred

## Executive summary

The strongest SEO strategy for this project is not scale-first publishing. It is **provenance-first topical authority**: own the exact New York custodian-exam intent cluster with original visual study content, dated county/exam profiles, concise explainers for logistics/scoring, and a lawful answer-seeking interception page.

The most important technical change from earlier SEO planning is that **Google removed Practice Problem structured-data support in January 2026**. `Quiz` remains only as a narrower Education Q&A/flashcard feature, not a generic exam/practice-test rich-result strategy.

There is also no defensible “minimum word count” for jurisdiction pages. Google explicitly says there is no preferred word count and separately prohibits doorway-like groups of substantially similar regional/query pages. County pages should therefore be gated by unique verified utility, not padded to a length target.

### Volume limitations

**Confirmed:** Public Google Trends data is normalized rather than an absolute search-count product. Keyword Planner can provide monthly search estimates but requires authenticated account/tool access. No defensible authenticated keyword-volume export was available in this pass.

**Inferred:** Use relative tiers until Search Console produces first-party impressions. Do not fabricate “X searches/month.” Once launched, Search Console becomes the most useful demand map because this niche is small and query phrasing is highly local/event-driven.

### Seasonality

**Confirmed:** Nassau administrations are irregular rather than annual: 2017, postponed 2020→2021, 2024, 2026. Filing windows, test dates, results, and eligible-list events create natural search triggers.

**Inferred:** Expect demand spikes around:
- announcement publication;
- filing midpoint/close;
- admission notices / T-14→T-1;
- test day;
- results/list establishment and appointments.

Do **not** create annual pages (`/2027/`, `/2028/`) without an actual announcement. Freshness should be event-triggered, not manufactured.

### Actual-questions / answers cluster

Live SERPs observed 2026-08-18 still contained Docsity, Stuvia, Studypool and similar pages using phrases such as “verified answers,” “actual questions,” “correct answers,” and “guaranteed pass.” These are low-provenance but highly intent-matched pages.

Recommended indexable page:

`/actual-custodian-exam-questions/`

It should immediately state:
- this site does not host secure, leaked, remembered or reconstructed exam items;
- officially published DCS samples are linked and explained;
- original cited practice is available free;
- why “actual questions/verified answers” pages cannot be treated as authoritative.

This intercepts the intent without imitating the bait.

### Featured snippet / concise-answer opportunities
Good short-answer shapes include:
- “Is 70 a 70% raw score?” → not necessarily; 70 refers to final rating and raw conversion is not established for this administration.
- “How long is the test?” → two hours for the series, per official DCS guide.
- “Are wrong answers penalized?” → general state guide says most covered tests do not deduct unless instructions say otherwise.
- “Can I use a calculator?” → quiet handheld calculator generally permitted unless candidates are notified otherwise; current notice controls.
- “Are these actual exam questions?” → no; original practice + officially released samples only.

Do not assume rich-result treatment; optimize for clear ordinary search snippets and user comprehension.

## Task 3 — Site architecture / programmatic pages

### Hub-and-spoke
Recommended hierarchy:

- `/ny/` — statewide Entry-Level Custodians & Janitors series hub
- `/ny/nassau-county/` — Nassau jurisdiction hub
- `/ny/nassau-county/custodian/60112026/` — immutable administration profile
- `/ny/nassau-county/custodian/61012026/` — separate promotion profile if needed in product IA
- `/atlas/`
- `/atlas/tool/{tool}/`
- `/atlas/family/{family}/`
- `/practice/`
- `/hazards/`
- `/print/`
- `/scoring/`
- `/actual-custodian-exam-questions/`
- `/es/...` — substantive Spanish equivalents

### Thin-content / doorway prevention

**Confirmed:** Google has no preferred minimum word count. Google doorway-abuse policy targets pages created for similar regional/query variants that funnel users to essentially the same destination.

Therefore there is **no numeric unique-content threshold**. Use a publish gate instead.

A jurisdiction page should be indexable only if it has real unique utility such as multiple of:
- a verified announcement or same-plan source;
- filing/test/result dates;
- jurisdiction/title eligibility distinctions;
- participating local entities;
- local official links;
- local results/list mechanics/status;
- substantive differences from statewide/default guidance.

If only the location name changes, do not index the page.

### Canonicalization
Canonical tags are not a doorway-page laundering mechanism. Near-duplicate jurisdiction pages should generally be consolidated/noindexed/not generated rather than produced en masse and canonicalized elsewhere.

Use self-canonicals on real unique pages. Shared study content should live on shared URLs rather than being duplicated inside every county path.

### Historical pages
Keep past exam-number pages permanently when they contain verified historical administration facts. After test day, change status rather than URL:
- past administration;
- results pending;
- list established;
- superseded by newer administration.

Link strongly to the current jurisdiction hub/new profile. Do not recycle the historical URL for a new year/exam.

### Internal linking
- Statewide prep page → current Nassau/local profile via a “taking this exam?” module.
- Local profile → shared study/practice/atlas.
- Atlas leaf → family/confusable comparisons + “practice this tool.”
- Practice result/explanation → atlas reference after commitment.
- Historical profile → current jurisdiction hub.
- Answer-seeking explainer → official sample guide + legitimate practice.

## Task 4 — E-E-A-T / trust with pseudonymous operator

A real-name author biography is not a prerequisite and should not be fabricated.

Priority trust signals:
1. **Per-fact citations to controlling .gov/public sources.**
2. **Visible last-verified dates** on changing facts.
3. **Stable project identity**, even if pseudonymous/organizational.
4. **Methodology/source hierarchy** explaining how claims are accepted.
5. **Corrections page + public correction history.**
6. **Explicit unknowns** instead of filling gaps.
7. **Independent/unofficial disclaimer** on relevant pages.
8. **Original-content/security policy** explaining no secure/recalled questions.
9. **Image provenance/rights records** for original art.
10. **Outbound links to official announcements/guides.**

### Outbound links
Normal editorial citations to official sources should generally be ordinary crawlable links. Linking to controlling sources helps users validate claims and is consistent with a transparent research site. Do not blanket `nofollow` trusted official citations; reserve `nofollow`, `sponsored`, and `ugc` for their actual purposes.

### About-page pattern
A good pseudonymous About page should explain:
- what the project is and is not;
- who maintains it at a stable project identity level;
- sourcing methodology;
- correction process;
- security/content policy;
- funding/ad policy (free, no ads if that remains true);
- contact route;
- lack of affiliation with DCS/Nassau/school districts.

## Task 5 — Technical SEO

### PWA/offline + JS
Indexable acquisition pages should have substantive HTML on initial response via SSG/SSR/hybrid rendering. Client-side JavaScript can hydrate:
- local progress;
- quizzes;
- timers;
- spaced review;
- offline pack state;
- answer reveal.

Do not require clicking/interaction for primary crawlable content on atlas/profile/reference pages.

Individual scored-session URLs are product state and usually should not be indexed.

### Core Web Vitals
Current “good” field thresholds at p75:
- LCP ≤ 2.5s
- INP ≤ 200ms
- CLS ≤ 0.1

For this audience, optimize for lower-end mobile/variable connectivity: keep acquisition pages lightweight, lazy-load noncritical art, cache immutable assets, and avoid JavaScript that delays the core explanatory content.

### Image SEO vs neutral quiz alt text
Use a page-type split.

**Atlas pages:**
- descriptive alt text;
- canonical English tool name in visible heading;
- full distinguishing-feature text;
- surrounding use/confusable explanation;
- descriptive asset URLs where security is irrelevant;
- image sitemap if useful;
- SVG originals; raster/social derivatives where needed.

**Scored pages:**
- neutral pre-submit description;
- no target name in hidden/accessible metadata;
- no revealing filename surfaced to client UI;
- full description only after commitment;
- generally not intended as image-search acquisition pages.

Google can index SVG images. The atlas, not the scored question player, should be the image-search acquisition surface.

### Structured data
- `BreadcrumbList`: yes on hierarchical indexable pages.
- `Organization`: yes if project identity fields are accurate.
- `FAQPage`: may be semantically valid but Google FAQ rich results are generally limited to authoritative government/health sites. Do not expect a rich result.
- `HowTo`: rich-result support deprecated; do not implement for SEO appearance.
- `QAPage`: do not use for site-authored practice/FAQ where users cannot submit answers.
- `Quiz`: Google's Education Q&A feature is a narrow flashcard/education implementation, not generic exam schema.
- **Practice Problem structured-data support was removed by Google in January 2026.** Do not implement it as an SEO feature.

### Multilingual
Use real language URLs and `hreflang` only for substantive translated counterparts. Self-referencing and reciprocal hreflang are preferred. Do not mass-index machine-translated thin county pages.

Translated slugs vs same slugs is primarily an editorial/UX choice; both can work. Consistency and stability matter more than forcing English keywords into Spanish URLs.

### Measurement
In Search Console maintain query/page regex groups for:
- prep/practice;
- logistics/date/results/list;
- scoring;
- answer-seeking;
- atlas/tool names;
- Nassau;
- other NY jurisdictions;
- Spanish;
- high-level/head-custodian;
- NYC-adjacent queries.

Take SERP/rank snapshots around announcement events rather than paying to monitor every tiny query daily.

## Task 6 — White-hat links/distribution

### Ranked targets
1. **Nassau Library System / member libraries** — link to Nassau hub or free practice hub. Pitch: free, cited, original practice and tool atlas for civil-service patrons.
2. **Town of Hempstead civil-service study-guides/resources** — Nassau hub/scoring/practice page.
3. **Suffolk/other NY public libraries with civil-service resource lists** — statewide hub when same-plan applicability is clear.
4. **NYSSFA / school-facilities organizations** — statewide hub/tool atlas as a free candidate resource, not as “official prep.”
5. **Union/local education resources (e.g. relevant CSEA/local pages)** — jurisdiction/profile or general free study hub.
6. **BOCES/adult-ed/classified-employment resources** — local or statewide hub depending audience.
7. **County/district candidate-facing pages** — only where their editorial policies allow external preparation resources.
8. **r/Custodians / Long Island/county communities** — participate normally; link only when directly useful to a discussion. No campaign-style mass posting.

### Honest pitch
“Free, independent, cited study material for the NYS Entry-Level Custodians & Janitors series, with original tool drawings and practice questions. It does not contain secure exam questions and links each factual explanation back to public sources.”

## Delta against the uploaded LANDSCAPE.md

### No contradiction — strengthened
1. **SEO-junk diagnosis is validated.** Live 2026 SERPs contain Docsity/Stuvia/Studypool pages using “verified answers,” “actual,” “latest,” and “guaranteed pass” language. LANDSCAPE’s exclusion policy should remain unchanged.
2. **Underserved-product thesis is validated.** Search still lacks a clear free NYS-series product combining original line-art recognition, hazard scenes, dated logistics, citations, offline/mobile operation, multilingual support and transparent provenance.
3. **Library distribution thesis is strengthened.** Town of Hempstead and Nassau/Suffolk library pages actively curate civil-service/test-prep links, making resource-list outreach realistic rather than hypothetical.
4. **Entry/high-level separation is SEO-critical as well as pedagogically critical.** Current `head custodian` and NYC Custodian Engineer SERPs are materially different clusters; blending them would both mislead users and muddy topical relevance.
5. **The existing two-mode alt-text design is correct.** SEO traffic should come through descriptive atlas pages; scored attempts should remain neutral and generally nonindexable.
6. **Event-driven profile architecture is correct.** Current evidence reinforces the FACTBASE rule that local exam cadence is irregular; profiles should be announcement-triggered, not yearly templated doorway pages.

### New additions LANDSCAPE should absorb
1. **No word-count threshold for jurisdiction pages.** Google explicitly says there is no preferred word count. Use a source/utility publish gate instead.
2. **Doorway-page prevention rule.** Do not generate indexable county pages whose only unique element is the county name or a link into shared practice.
3. **Historical profile retention rule.** Keep immutable exam-number URLs after administration and change status/CTA rather than recycling dates.
4. **PWA rendering rule.** All acquisition pages should be SSG/SSR/hybrid with substantive initial HTML; session interactivity remains client-side.
5. **Structured-data update (important): Practice Problem rich results were removed in January 2026.** Do not plan around them.
6. **`Quiz` nuance:** current Google support is the Education Q&A flashcard feature, not general exam/practice-test markup.
7. **FAQ/HowTo limits:** no expectation of FAQ rich results for this independent site; HowTo rich results are deprecated.
8. **Outbound-link rule:** normal followed links to trusted official sources are desirable; blanket nofollow is counterproductive.
9. **Pseudonymous authorship rule:** use a stable real project/pseudonymous identity plus methodology, citations, corrections, dates and source hierarchy; do not fabricate credentialed author personas.
10. **Search Console intent segmentation:** prep, logistics, answer-seeking, image, Spanish, Nassau, high-level, and NYC-adjacent traffic should be measured separately.

### Direct contradictions found
**None in the uploaded LANDSCAPE.md.** The meaningful change is not a contradiction with LANDSCAPE; it is a correction to the **previous SEO report**: Practice Problem structured data is no longer a supported Google Search feature as of January 2026.
