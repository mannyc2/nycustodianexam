## Scene contract

Support:

- unannotated controlling static scene;
- no target-count leak;
- intended hazards;
- documented safe-but-suspicious decoys;
- zero accidental hazards;
- normalized zones/regions;
- post-answer overlays/key;
- stable zone reading order;
- print worksheet/key;
- nonvisual equivalent;
- immutable scene version.

## Generative boundary

If generative methods are retained, specify:

- project-owned inputs only;
- immutable model/input hashes;
- fixed model/version/seed/sampler;
- target/decoy inventory;
- depth/segmentation/keypoint/silhouette validation;
- no new unauthored objects/hazards;
- independent accidental-hazard reviewer;
- deterministic pre-generation render as controlling fallback;
- rejection thresholds.

Do not make model output the authority for safety facts.

## Modular asset architecture

Evaluate a reusable library of rooms, fixtures, cords, doors, exits, ladders, containers, carts, fire equipment, people/poses, spills, glass, PPE, and signs. Avoid template repetition that teaches scene-location shortcuts.

- hazard inventory/meaning;

- decoy;
- hotspot;
- camera/composition;
- description;
- render-only cleanup;
- compression.

- damaged cord/grounding;
- ladder misuse;

- lifting/material handling;
- broken glass/sharps;
