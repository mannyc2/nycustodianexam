# Research corpus map

These files are the canonical project knowledge base.

| Document | Use |
|---|---|
| [`FACTBASE.md`](FACTBASE.md) | Established facts about the series, administrations, scoring, logistics, legal framework, titles, and jurisdictions. |
| [`SCOPE.md`](SCOPE.md) | Source-backed facts suitable for defining what original study content may cover. |
| [`TAXONOMY.md`](TAXONOMY.md) | Tool, equipment, hazard, confusion-pair, and evidence-tier taxonomy. |
| [`LANDSCAPE.md`](LANDSCAPE.md) | Competitive landscape, learning evidence, accessibility/design research, and product strategy. |
| [`OPEN.md`](OPEN.md) | Unresolved questions, contradictions, recovery targets, and pending decisions. |

## Precedence

Do not infer project truth from filenames alone. Read the relevant document's status and dates. In general, newer controlling official evidence supersedes older official evidence, and official evidence outranks commercial or anecdotal material.

## Derived material

Files in `../research/` are research outputs or proposals. They do not become canonical merely by being committed. Accepted findings should be reconciled into the documents above.

The prompts in `../prompts/` are reusable research instructions and likewise are not evidence themselves.
