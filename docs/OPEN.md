## High-priority unresolved items

### C1 — Who actually prepares the Nassau Custodian examination?
**Question:** Is the Nassau Custodian examination formally prepared by NYS DCS under Civil Service Law §23(2), or by Nassau using state materials/services under another arrangement?

**Why it matters:** Authorship controls the strongest claims that can be made about review/appeal authority, custody, question provenance, and statewide form identity.

**Known:** Nassau announcements consistently link the NYS DCS Entry-Level Custodians and Janitors guide; subject wording is materially identical across administrations and other jurisdictions. Nassau rules provide that when a state-prepared §23(2) examination is used, DCS corrects ratings and state review/appeal rules control. This makes DCS preparation probable, but the announcement language itself does not expressly identify the preparer.

**Resolve with:** Nassau↔DCS §23(2) examination-service request, transmittal, invoice/contract, correspondence, or other record naming the preparer for a Custodian administration.

**Status:** OPEN — probable DCS preparation; do not elevate to confirmed authorship without direct record.

### C2 — Exact official item count / domain weighting / score conversion
**Question:** How many scored items are on the entry-level Custodian exam, how are the three subject areas weighted, are there unscored/pretest items, and how is raw performance converted to the final rating?

**Why it matters:** Prevents false “full-length,” weighted-score, pass-probability, and raw-percent claims.

**Known:** Two-hour multiple-choice written exam; minimum final rating generally 70 unless otherwise announced. Final rating is not established to equal raw percent correct. No controlling source located for item count, equal weighting, conversion formula, or unscored items.

**Resolve with:** controlling rating key/technical manual, state/local examination administration documentation, released scoring guidance, or a lawful official post-administration record that expressly answers these questions.

**Status:** OPEN. Product must continue selectable site-designed lengths and “practice accuracy only.”
