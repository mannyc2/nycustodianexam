## Format & logistics
- MC written exam; paper booklet + machine-scannable answer sheet, computer-scored (per series + general guides — best-supported expectation; admission notice controls); two hours for this series; drawings not necessarily to scale; No. 2 pencils; photo ID; booklet/answer sheet are DCS property. [OFFICIAL]
- Calculator: quiet handheld solar/battery permitted unless candidates are notified otherwise; keyboard devices, electronic dictionaries/translators/spell-checkers/address books prohibited; books/notes/reference materials prohibited. [OFFICIAL]
- Cross-filers: total same-date testing capped at 8 hours. [OFFICIAL]
- Written test is the only announced competitive component; post-list medical/psych/fitness/fingerprint/background steps are appointment conditions. NYSED fingerprinting rules distinguish workers with direct student contact from those without while school-district appointment practices vary. [OFFICIAL]
- Exam-location/admission-notice logistics are administration-specific; general guide says candidate must bring notice and government-issued photo ID. [OFFICIAL]

## Scoring, ratings, review, lists
- Minimum passing final rating is generally 70 unless otherwise announced; this is a converted final rating, not necessarily raw percent-correct. [OFFICIAL]
- Wrong-answer penalty: general guide says most tests do not deduct; answer every item unless announcement says otherwise. [OFFICIAL]
- Veterans credits are added to final passing score after eligibility proof, not to raw answers. [OFFICIAL]
- Seniority credits apply to promotion exams as provided by statute/rule and announcement. [OFFICIAL]
- Nassau local list administration follows Civil Service Law and county rules; appointment depends on reachable eligible-list status and appointing-authority selection, not simply "passing." [OFFICIAL]
- Review procedures depend on whether DCS or Nassau prepared the exam. For a DCS-prepared §23(2) exam, DCS review/appeal rules control. [OFFICIAL]
