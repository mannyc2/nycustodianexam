Settings — two preferences and everything you can do with your own data

Small on purpose. Three things a learner can change, four things they can do with what they have saved, and an honest list of what this page cannot do yet. The prototype’s startup destination and automatic text-equivalent preference are gone — no code reads either.

Calls I made here

  • No Save button. Larger text and reduced motion change the page the moment you set them, so a Save that appears to be pending would be the only untrue thing on the screen. Each row carries its own quiet status, and a failed write puts the control back the way it was and says so in the same row.
  • Language is a statement, not a control. One published language means no picker to fake. The row says English, and Spanish is marked not available with the reason beside it. A spanishAvailable tweak shows the row as the select it becomes when a second language is reviewed.
  • Four data actions, one row template. Export, import, rebuild the review queue and delete are task cards with the glyph, name, sentence, action template, so the actions align. Export offers the correction drafts as a checkbox inside the card rather than as a second card.
  • Destructive means preview, then confirm, and export offered first. Import and delete both run choose → preview → confirm, and the preview says in its heading that nothing has been written. Delete is scoped: the preview counts exactly what each scope would take.
  • Not available is on the page, not hidden. Low-data mode and Spanish content are the two things learners ask for that do not exist; they sit in a hairline table with a reason each. Startup destination and automatic text equivalents were prototype inventions and moved to the future section instead — nobody asked for them.

The four flows, step by step

Each panel opens in place under the card it belongs to, at 38rem — the width it has to survive on a narrow screen. Import and delete both go choose, preview, confirm.

  • Export — what the file will hold, before you make it

    Export my progress

    A single file saved wherever your browser puts downloads. It is yours: nothing is uploaded, and importing it on another device gives you this history there.

    Included
    23 sessions, 214 attempts, your review list, your exam choice, and these settings
    Not included
    The offline copies themselves. They are 18.4 MB each and can be downloaded again on the other device.

  • Storage unavailable — the whole data section says so once, not four times
    ▲

    This device is not letting the site read or write anything

    Export, import, rebuilding and deleting all need saved data, so none of them can run and no count can be shown. This does not mean your work is gone — it means it cannot be reached right now.

    Private browsing and a full disk are the two usual causes. Reading and motion still work; they just will not be remembered after you close the tab.
