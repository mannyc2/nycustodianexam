# Project rules — NYC Custodian Component Design

Owner decisions that apply to every design in this project. They sit on top of the repo contracts (`product/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`, `product/CONTENT_DESIGN.md` `CL-CODEX-1`, `product/ROUTES.md`).

## Banned styling (2026-09-02)

- No uppercase, letter-spaced "eyebrow" labels (the `YOUR EXAM` pattern) and no uppercase status chips (`VERIFIED`). Sentence case everywhere, no `text-transform: uppercase`, no tracking. Labels may be bold and action-coloured; mono is fine for dates and identifiers but never uppercased or tracked.
- No metadata strings that chain agency, competition type, series and identifiers with dashes or middle dots — e.g. `Nassau County Civil Service Commission — open competitive, entry-level series · exam 60112026`. Write a sentence, or put each item in its own labelled slot.
- Exam numbers, registry versions, fact-sheet line ids, effective dates, checksums and route paths belong under a native `Technical details` disclosure, never in the default layer (CONTENT_DESIGN.md `CL-LAYER-PROOF`). There is no exception: the obsolete allowance for a registry version inside a header exam chip is withdrawn (2026-09-07) because no exam chip exists.
- Do not stamp "Verified" on every fact. Mark only the states that change the reading — Not published, Superseded, Not applicable, Conflicting, Unverified — with the reason beside the label. Proof is one plain line per record: "Where this comes from: publisher, document, date."
- U.S. English: "August 22, 2026", practice (verb), recognizing, program.

## Direction (2026-09-02)

- The owner prefers the structure of `Landing.dc.html` turn 1 (dark prominent header with a figures strip, section headers, timeline cards, master–detail record list with tabs, hairline fact tables, task cards, dark footer). Those patterns are promoted to primitives in `Component Library.dc.html` §f-landing and `styles-additions.css`; build registry and landing pages from them rather than from prose-only cards.
- `Landing.dc.html` is route family 2, `/exams/` (`exam-selector`).
- Route decision (2026-09-02): `/` (`home`, family 1) is a public visitor landing — static overview and navigation on the turn-1 structure (dark prominent header, figures strip, task cards, dark footer); no pill is current there, the wordmark is Home. The study dashboard with resume/due summaries is `/practice/` (`study-hub`, family 5) behind the Study pill — that design is `Study Hub.dc.html` (formerly `Home.dc.html`). Both are designed on the turn-1 structure (`Home.dc.html`; `Study Hub.dc.html` turn 2).
- Canonical form (2026-09-02, tightened 2026-09-07): one DC per route family holding the settled variant only. Explorations no longer sit in an archive section below it — they move to a separate archive DC in `exports/explorations/` with a dated “Archive — not an implementation reference” banner, because a withdrawn pattern inside a shipped file gets read as a target. Library specimens do not count as page designs. Applied 2026-09-07: `Study Hub.dc.html` and `Landing.dc.html` hold only their settled design; their earlier turns are `exports/explorations/Study Hub - archive.dc.html` and `Landing - archive.dc.html`.
- Breadcrumbs (2026-09-03): only on nested reference pages (DESIGN_SYSTEM.md §242, e.g. the Atlas record shell), styled as the SiteNav pill. Top-level lanes rely on the current nav pill; none on Home, Study or Exams.
- TaskCard rows use a fixed template (glyph, name, sentence, action) so actions align across a row.
- EmptyState (2026-09-03) is the one standard for a list with zero records: a dashed slot (`1px dashed #667085`) exactly where the list would be, a heading that names what is absent in the learner’s words (never the word “empty”, no period), one sentence on what puts items there, at most two actions (secondary by default; primary only when nothing else on the page performs that action, e.g. choosing an exam). Three occasions: nothing recorded yet, filtered to nothing (scope in the heading, first action clears it), cleared (success, no green). Heading has `tabindex="-1"` and is focused only after the learner’s own filter, search or completion produced the state. Never used for unavailable, read-failed, offline-unavailable or withdrawn — those are Notice / PageState compositions with a solid border. Specimen: `Component Library.dc.html` §f-empty; classes `.empty-state*` in `styles-additions.css`.
- Offline sequence (2026-09-05, amended 2026-09-07): the pack controls are download and check → turn on → preview removal → confirm removal, and nothing else. There is no Pause, no Cancel and no resumable transfer: an interrupted download starts again from the beginning and a part-written copy is never kept or turned on, so no `paused-offline`-style state may promise stored progress. Learner labels are “Not downloaded”, “Downloading”, “Checking download”, “Ready to turn on”, “Checking before turning on”, “Turned on”, “Kept for older work”, “Did not pass its check”, “Removing” — record names stay in the Component Library specimen only. Removal previews before it acts: one preview screen states the impact in the counts the product can compute (active sessions that need this exact copy; saved attempts that stay in history but may lose their content) and carries the confirm button beside Keep it — it never names individual sessions or attempts, and removal is withheld entirely when an active session pins that exact copy. `Offline.dc.html` is route family 20, `/offline/`.
- Settings (2026-09-05): no Save button — each preference writes as it is set, with a quiet per-row status, and a failed write restores the control and states the reason in that row. Only larger text and reduced motion exist. Language is a labelled value, not a select, while only `en-US` is published. Low-data mode and Spanish content are named on the page as not available with a reason each; startup destination (“Open on”) and an automatic text-equivalent preference are not supported and live only in the future section. Import and scoped delete both run choose → preview → confirm, the preview heading says nothing has been written, and export is offered before any destructive scope. `Settings.dc.html` is route family 19, `/settings/`.
- Future functionality (2026-09-06): capability the implementation does not have lives on one separate page, `Future Explorations.dc.html` — no route, grouped by lane, each card a real drawing inside a dashed `#b7c1cc` frame on `#f7f8fa`, closed by a “Why it is out” line and a “To bring it back / Worth revisiting when” line. Route DCs stay limited to working capability: no disabled teaser controls, no greyed-out switches, no `#future` section of their own — at most one sentence naming what was removed and pointing here.
- Practice set length (2026-09-06): 45 questions is the lead length everywhere (Study hub, Session Builder, Question Player) because it is the smallest published set; 60 and 90 stay available beside it. The 10-question set is gone from every current-release screen and default and survives only as a future design.
- Exam dates and uncertainty (2026-09-06): keep the verified milestones with their source and last-checked date, label the administration “Not confirmed”, and leave next-cycle dates unspecified. Never make a blanket “nothing is open for filing” claim the catalog cannot establish — say what was read, when, and that later filing may exist unrecorded here.
- Review rows (2026-09-06): “Read explanation” is the primary row action and never completes the review; “Finish review” is a separate quieter button and removes the item from the ready queue only after confirmation. Scope tabs are All / Missed / Flagged and may overlap; there is no due-today/tomorrow schedule and no “Corrected” reason.
- Unavailable saved attempts (2026-09-06): a saved attempt that cannot be read goes under an “Unavailable saved attempts” heading in a solid neutral notice reading “This saved attempt can’t be displayed.” Show the metadata that is known, preserve the record, and invent nothing — no cause, no correction badge, no withdrawal fact, no Finish review action.
- HistoryList (2026-09-03, retargeted 2026-09-07 from sessions to saved records): one row template — mono date, record label with its kind, what was saved, one link. Up to six rows: plain list. Seven or more: month groups and a footer that counts (“Showing 6 of 23 saved activities.”) and expands in place. Twelve or more: a kind filter as the Tabs row (full-width hairline, selected kind underlined in action blue, `role="tablist"`) with plain counts beside the labels — never the boxed FilterGroup, which is reserved for a toolbar beside a search field (exam registry). Never a percentage or a score in a row. Specimen: §f-history.
- Practice hub for a first visit (settled): the header is the first action (“Start with a set of 45.”), the figures strip carries site facts (90 questions, 18 scenes, 65 tool records, 3 areas) rather than zeros, What practice covers reads next, Ways to practice follows, Saved activity keeps its three rows with “No saved answers in this release” so the page does not reorganize after the first answer, and Ready for review / Recent activity are EmptyStates.
- Navigation (2026-09-06): three destinations and one utility — Practice, Library, Exams, and Settings alone on the right. Sources is footer-only plus the “Where this comes from” line on each record; Offline is reached from Settings and from download or recovery actions, never from the header. Library is a native `details`/`summary` disclosure, not a route: three rows — Tool atlas, Tool comparisons, Hazard scenes — each a bold name and one sentence carrying its count, closed by a “What practice covers” row; below 48rem the same list is a sheet above a four-tab bar (Practice, Library, Exams, Settings) and nothing is called Menu or More.
- No active-exam selection (2026-09-06): the product never stores a chosen exam, so no interface may imply one — no exam chip, no “Choose your exam”, no “pins the facts” copy, no “Your exam” heading, no per-exam scoping of content. Exam pages are reading: they state what an announcement says and when it was read. The practice hub states which material every set draws on.
- Practice scope (2026-09-06): “Study” is now Practice and it opens `/practice/` — resume, ways to practice, saved work, and the scope statement. One question bank of 90 items covers the three announced subject areas for the whole entry-level custodians and janitors series; set length (45, 60, 90) changes how many questions you answer, never where they come from. `/practice/#covers` (“What practice covers”) holds the long form: one panel naming the three areas with its source line, one panel of what it does not do (does not know your exam, no announcement-specific or secure material, counts are never a score), and a “Compare with your announcement” link to Exams.
- Setup screens (2026-09-06): a builder offers only choices that visibly change the activity — subject areas, question count, scene count and task construct, timing where it exists — and never a profile or exam picker whose options produce identical practice. Availability is computed from the choice (a shortfall notice states the real count and never pads with repeats; no areas selected disables Start). Exam identifiers do not appear, not even under Technical details; a session pins a content pack version only.
- Placement of secondary information (2026-09-06): storage sits with saved work (“kept in this browser on this device”, with Export or erase and Keep a copy offline beside Recent activity), accessibility choices sit beside the activity (larger text and reduced motion linked from Ways to practice), counts appear where they help someone choose (inside nav-row and card sentences), and offline reassurance appears at most once per page — never as a repeated tagline, never chained with middle dots.

## Saved records and review (2026-09-07)

- The saved record is an attempt, not a session. The product keeps one record per answered question, one per scene response and one per finished review; there is no session object, so nothing may resume a session, score one, or list sessions. `/practice/` shows **Recent activity**: one row per record — mono date, label (`Question 12`, `Hazard scene 3`, `Review · question 9`), its kind, what was saved (`Answer saved`, `Answer saved · flagged`, `Visual response saved`, `Keyboard response saved`, `Review finished`) and a Feedback link back to the explanation; a record whose attempt cannot be read reads “Explanation unavailable” instead. Kind tabs (All activity / Questions / Hazards / Reviews) appear at twelve rows or more; six rows show by default under month labels with “Showing 6 of 23 saved activities.”
- No accuracy anywhere. Nothing computes a percentage, a weekly total or a per-area figure, so “Where you stand”, “Answered this week” and “Practice accuracy” are withdrawn. `/practice/` states **Saved activity** instead — question practice, hazard scanning and tool reference, each with what is saved — and the subject areas keep their single home in What practice covers. First-visit figures carry site facts (90 questions, 18 scenes, 65 tools, 3 areas); returning figures carry counts (questions answered, scene responses, finished reviews, unavailable saved attempts).
- Review is ready, not due. There is no schedule: an item is ready until the learner finishes it. Figures are Ready for review, Missed or misidentified, Flagged by you — never due today, due tomorrow, reviewed this week, or a rebuilt-from count in the default layer. Scopes All / Missed / Flagged overlap by design and the status line says how many the current scope shows. Nothing is ever queued for having been corrected after an attempt, and there is no Corrected scope or reason.
- Review reasons are the five authored sentences, verbatim: “You answered this question incorrectly.”, “You flagged this question for review.”, “You missed a hazard in this scene.”, “You marked a detail that is safe as shown in this scene.”, “You marked a spot where this scene records no hazard.” No chips, no counts per reason inside a card.
- Finishing a review happens on `/review/` only. “Read explanation” is the primary row action and never completes anything; “Finish review” opens a confirmation group inside the card (“Finish this review?”, heading focused) whose Confirm removes the item and whose “Keep in review” returns focus to the button. The player has no Mark reviewed, no session summary and no review completion of its own.
- Practice player controls are the flag toggle, the answer choices, Save answer, and after the answer is saved the explanation, its sources, Open study tools and Report a correction. Nothing else is drawn as a player control.
- Settings counts are event records (question answers + scene responses + finished reviews), never sessions or attempts-plus-sessions: export names “23 saved event records”, the rebuild receipt reads “Read 19 attempts: 5 ready for review; 0 could not be checked. History unchanged.”, and the delete preview counts records in the chosen scope with “Offline downloads are not included.”

## Section headers (2026-09-07)

- A section header is a heading, on its own line. The split layout — `h2` on the left and a paragraph pushed to the right edge by `justify-content: space-between` — is withdrawn everywhere: it read as two unrelated columns and left a ragged gap between them.
- Most of those paragraphs were filler and are deleted ("The short version. Each row links to the full page.", "Pick an entry to read its announcement facts", "One read tells you whether these sets are the right practice"). Delete by default.
- Keep one only when it states a fact or a caveat the section body does not — the announcement governs, items stay ready until you finish them, what was saved is never a score — and then set it as a subheader directly under the heading: `<div style="display: grid; gap: 0.375rem; justify-items: start;">` with the heading, then one `<p>` at `0.9375rem`, `#4b5563`, `max-width: 74ch`. Never two sentences where one will do.
- A link row that used to ride along on the right (larger text and reduced motion, export or erase, keep a copy offline) stays in that stack, below the heading and its subheader, as a `flex` row with `gap: 0.25rem 1rem`.
