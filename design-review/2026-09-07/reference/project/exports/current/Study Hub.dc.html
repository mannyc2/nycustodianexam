Study hub — before the first set, and after the twenty-third

Two ends of the same page. A learner who has never studied gets a hub that starts them rather than reports on them: the header is the first action, the ways to study sit directly under it, the three areas say what they cover, and the two lists that would be empty stand as dashed EmptyStates that say what will fill them. A learner twenty-three sessions in gets Recent sessions as its own section — one row template, month groups, a footer that counts and expands, a filter by kind — so the list reads the same at four rows and at forty.

Calls I made here

  • Start, not status. With nothing recorded, the figures strip carries what is here to study — 3 areas, 65 tool records, 18 scenes — instead of four zeros, and Ways to study moves up under the header. Where you stand keeps its name and its three rows, each saying what the area covers and “No attempts yet”, so the page does not reorganize after the first set; the rows just gain their bars.
  • Empty is a standard now. Due today and Recent sessions render the EmptyState: dashed border, a heading that names what is absent, one sentence on what creates it, at most two actions. The exam slot is the same component carrying the one primary action nothing else on the page performs. Unavailable is never dashed: the read-failed state (tweak) puts a warning notice in the same slots.
  • Recent sessions scales. Rows are date, session, outcome, link. Six show by default under month labels with “Showing the 6 most recent of 23”; Show all expands in place. A filter by kind sits above; filtering to Simulations, of which there are none, produces the filtered EmptyState with its heading focused. Under seven sessions none of this machinery appears.
  • Figures follow the history. Twenty-three sessions means 690 questions answered and 40 hazard scenes, so the strip and the area bars are recomputed from the list rather than carried over from the four-session version.
  • Unreadable is not missing. Two saved attempts that cannot be opened sit under their own “Unavailable saved attempts” heading in a solid-border list, each reading “This saved attempt can’t be displayed” with the date and length that are known. No cause is invented, they stay counted in the figures, and neither offers an action.
