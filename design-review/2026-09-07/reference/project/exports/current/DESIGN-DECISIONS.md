# Project rules — NYC Custodian Component Design

Owner decisions that apply to every design in this project. They sit on top of the repo contracts (`product/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`, `product/CONTENT_DESIGN.md` `CL-CODEX-1`, `product/ROUTES.md`).

## Banned styling (2026-09-02)

- No uppercase, letter-spaced "eyebrow" labels (the `YOUR EXAM` pattern) and no uppercase status chips (`VERIFIED`). Sentence case everywhere, no `text-transform: uppercase`, no tracking. Labels may be bold and action-coloured; mono is fine for dates and identifiers but never uppercased or tracked.
- No metadata strings that chain agency, competition type, series and identifiers with dashes or middle dots — e.g. `Nassau County Civil Service Commission — open competitive, entry-level series · exam 60112026`. Write a sentence, or put each item in its own labelled slot.
- Exam numbers, registry versions, fact-sheet line ids, effective dates, checksums and route paths belong under a native `Technical details` disclosure, never in the default layer (CONTENT_DESIGN.md `CL-LAYER-PROOF`). The only exception is the registry version inside the header exam chip, which ROUTES.md requires.
- Do not stamp "Verified" on every fact. Mark only the states that change the reading — Not published, Superseded, Not applicable, Conflicting, Unverified — with the reason beside the label. Proof is one plain line per record: "Where this comes from: publisher, document, date."
- U.S. English: "August 22, 2026", practice (verb), recognizing, program.

## Direction (2026-09-02)

- The owner prefers the structure of `Landing.dc.html` turn 1 (dark prominent header with a figures strip, section headers with a side note, timeline cards, master–detail record list with tabs, hairline fact tables, task cards, dark footer). Those patterns are promoted to primitives in `Component Library.dc.html` §f-landing and `styles-additions.css`; build registry and landing pages from them rather than from prose-only cards.
- `Landing.dc.html` is route family 2, `/exams/` (`exam-selector`).
- Route decision (2026-09-02): `/` (`home`, family 1) is a public visitor landing — static overview and navigation on the turn-1 structure (dark prominent header, figures strip, task cards, dark footer); no pill is current there, the wordmark is Home. The study dashboard with resume/due summaries is `/practice/` (`study-hub`, family 5) behind the Study pill — that design is `Study Hub.dc.html` (formerly `Home.dc.html`). Both are designed on the turn-1 structure (`Home.dc.html`; `Study Hub.dc.html` turn 2).
- Canonical form: one DC per route family holding the settled variant only; explorations move to an archive section below it. Library specimens do not count as page designs. Applied 2026-09-03: `Landing.dc.html` (settled + turn 1 archived), `Study Hub.dc.html` (turn 3 settled; turns 2 and 1 archived).
- Breadcrumbs (2026-09-03): only on nested reference pages (DESIGN_SYSTEM.md §242, e.g. the Atlas record shell), styled as the SiteNav pill. Top-level lanes rely on the current nav pill; none on Home, Study or Exams.
- TaskCard rows use a fixed template (glyph, name, sentence, action) so actions align across a row.
- EmptyState (2026-09-03) is the one standard for a list with zero records: a dashed slot (`1px dashed #667085`) exactly where the list would be, a heading that names what is absent in the learner’s words (never the word “empty”, no period), one sentence on what puts items there, at most two actions (secondary by default; primary only when nothing else on the page performs that action, e.g. choosing an exam). Three occasions: nothing recorded yet, filtered to nothing (scope in the heading, first action clears it), cleared (success, no green). Heading has `tabindex="-1"` and is focused only after the learner’s own filter, search or completion produced the state. Never used for unavailable, read-failed, offline-unavailable or withdrawn — those are Notice / PageState compositions with a solid border. Specimen: `Component Library.dc.html` §f-empty; classes `.empty-state*` in `styles-additions.css`.
- Offline sequence (2026-09-05): the pack controls are download and check → turn on → preview removal → confirm removal, and nothing else. There is no Pause, no Cancel and no resumable transfer: an interrupted download starts again from the beginning and a part-written copy is never kept or turned on, so no `paused-offline`-style state may promise stored progress. Learner labels are “Not downloaded”, “Downloading”, “Ready to turn on”, “Turned on”, “Kept for older work”, “Did not pass its check”, “Update did not finish”, “Cannot be removed yet” — record names stay in the Component Library specimen only. Removal is always two screens (preview naming exact dependent sessions and attempts, then confirm) and is withheld entirely when a paused session pins that exact copy. `Offline.dc.html` is route family 20, `/offline/`.
- Settings (2026-09-05): no Save button — each preference writes as it is set, with a quiet per-row status, and a failed write restores the control and states the reason in that row. Only larger text and reduced motion exist. Language is a labelled value, not a select, while only `en-US` is published. Low-data mode and Spanish content are named on the page as not available with a reason each; startup destination (“Open on”) and an automatic text-equivalent preference are not supported and live only in the future section. Import and scoped delete both run choose → preview → confirm, the preview heading says nothing has been written, and export is offered before any destructive scope. `Settings.dc.html` is route family 19, `/settings/`.
- Future functionality (2026-09-06): capability the implementation does not have lives on one separate page, `Future Explorations.dc.html` — no route, grouped by lane, each card a real drawing inside a dashed `#b7c1cc` frame on `#f7f8fa`, closed by a “Why it is out” line and a “To bring it back / Worth revisiting when” line. Route DCs stay limited to working capability: no disabled teaser controls, no greyed-out switches, no `#future` section of their own — at most one sentence naming what was removed and pointing here.
- Practice set length (2026-09-06): 45 questions is the lead length everywhere (Study hub, Session Builder, Question Player) because it is the smallest published set; 60 and 90 stay available beside it. The 10-question set is gone from every current-release screen and default and survives only as a future design.
- Exam dates and uncertainty (2026-09-06): keep the verified milestones with their source and last-checked date, label the administration “Not confirmed”, and leave next-cycle dates unspecified. Never make a blanket “nothing is open for filing” claim the catalog cannot establish — say what was read, when, and that later filing may exist unrecorded here.
- Review rows (2026-09-06): “Read explanation” is the primary row action and never completes the review; “Finish review” is a separate quieter button and removes the item from the ready queue only after confirmation. Scope tabs are All / Missed / Flagged and may overlap; there is no due-today/tomorrow schedule and no “Corrected” reason.
- Unavailable saved attempts (2026-09-06): a saved attempt that cannot be read goes under an “Unavailable saved attempts” heading in a solid neutral notice reading “This saved attempt can’t be displayed.” Show the metadata that is known, preserve the record, and invent nothing — no cause, no correction badge, no withdrawal fact, no Finish review action.
- Recent sessions (HistoryList, 2026-09-03): one row template — mono date, session name with one sentence of scope, outcome as a count (“7 of 10 correct”, “12 of 18 answered”), one link (Resume or Results). Up to six sessions: plain list. Seven or more: month groups and a footer that counts (“Showing the 6 most recent of 23”) and expands in place. Twelve or more: a kind filter as the Tabs row (full-width hairline, selected kind underlined in action blue, `role="tablist"`) with plain counts beside the labels — never the boxed FilterGroup, which is reserved for a toolbar beside a search field (exam registry). Never a percentage or a score in a row. Specimen: §f-history.
- Study hub for a first visit (turn 3): the header is the first action (“Start with a set of 45.”), the figures strip carries site facts (3 areas, 65 tool records, 18 scenes, exam not chosen) rather than zeros, Ways to study sits directly under the header, Where you stand keeps its three rows with “No attempts yet” so the page does not reorganize after the first set, and Due today / Recent sessions / Your exam are EmptyStates.
- Navigation (2026-09-06): three destinations and one utility — Practice, Library, Exams, and Settings alone on the right. Sources is footer-only plus the “Where this comes from” line on each record; Offline is reached from Settings and from download or recovery actions, never from the header. Library is a native `details`/`summary` disclosure, not a route: three rows — Tool atlas, Tool comparisons, Hazard scenes — each a bold name and one sentence carrying its count, closed by a “What practice covers” row; below 48rem the same list is a sheet above a four-tab bar (Practice, Library, Exams, Settings) and nothing is called Menu or More.
- No active-exam selection (2026-09-06): the product never stores a chosen exam, so no interface may imply one — no exam chip, no “Choose your exam”, no “pins the facts” copy, no “Your exam” heading, no per-exam scoping of content. Exam pages are reading: they state what an announcement says and when it was read. The practice hub states which material every set draws on.
- Practice scope (2026-09-06): “Study” is now Practice and it opens `/practice/` — resume, ways to practice, saved work, and the scope statement. One question bank of 90 items covers the three announced subject areas for the whole entry-level custodians and janitors series; set length (45, 60, 90) changes how many questions you answer, never where they come from. `/practice/#covers` (“What practice covers”) holds the long form: one panel naming the three areas with its source line, one panel of what it does not do (does not know your exam, no announcement-specific or secure material, counts are never a score), and a “Compare with your announcement” link to Exams.
- Setup screens (2026-09-06): a builder offers only choices that visibly change the activity — subject areas, question count, scene count and task construct, timing where it exists — and never a profile or exam picker whose options produce identical practice. Availability is computed from the choice (a shortfall notice states the real count and never pads with repeats; no areas selected disables Start). Exam identifiers do not appear, not even under Technical details; a session pins a content pack version only.
- Placement of secondary information (2026-09-06): storage sits with saved work (“kept in this browser on this device”, with Export or erase and Keep a copy offline beside Recent sessions), accessibility choices sit beside the activity (larger text and reduced motion linked from Ways to study), counts appear where they help someone choose (inside nav-row and card sentences), and offline reassurance appears at most once per page — never as a repeated tagline, never chained with middle dots.
