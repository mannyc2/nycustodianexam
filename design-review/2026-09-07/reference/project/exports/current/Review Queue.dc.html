Review queue — what is ready, and why

The page “Review your saved work” lands on. Same structure as Practice one level down: the dark header says how much is ready and opens the first explanation, the figures are the learner’s own, and the queue itself is a list of item cards scoped by a row of tabs. An item is ready until the learner finishes it — nothing is due on a date, and reading an explanation is not finishing.

Calls I made here

  • Ready, not due. There is no review schedule, so “7 items due today”, “due tomorrow” and “reviewed this week” are gone. The figures are the three the queue can actually state: how many items are ready, how many are missed or misidentified, and how many the learner flagged.
  • The scopes overlap on purpose. All, Missed and Flagged are the Tabs row; one item can appear under both Missed and Flagged, and the status line says how many the current scope shows. There is no Corrected scope — a correction never queues an item.
  • Two actions, one of them destructive. “Read explanation” is the primary action on every card and never completes the review. “Finish review” is quieter and opens a confirmation in place, with its heading focused; confirming is what removes the item.
  • Reasons are sentences. Each card lists the authored reasons in plain words — you answered incorrectly, you flagged this, you missed a hazard, you marked a safe detail — instead of a chip or a count.
  • Clear is success; unreadable is not empty. A cleared queue is a plain statement with somewhere to go next. A failed read replaces the header with a recovery notice offering Retry, Rebuild and Export, states that nothing was deleted, and keeps the review history, which is read from the attempt events that survived.
  • Unreadable attempts get their own list. A saved attempt that cannot be read is named, dated and left alone under “Unavailable saved attempts” — no cause, no correction badge, no action.

How review works

What gets queued
Missed and flagged questions, missed visual hazards, and safe details marked as hazards. An item can be both missed and flagged, so the scopes overlap. Nothing is queued for a date, and nothing is inferred from how long you took.
When you finish
Reading an explanation never removes an item. Confirm Finish review to take it out of your ready queue; your original answer and its explanation stay in your history.
If an attempt is unavailable
It stays in your saved history with the information that can be read, and it is not queued. Nothing is deleted and no cause is guessed.
Saved in this browser
The queue is rebuilt from the attempts saved on this device. Export a backup before clearing browser data.
