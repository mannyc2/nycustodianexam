Home — the public front door

The page a stranger lands on. It says what the site is, what the test covers, how to study for it and where the 2026 cycle stands — then hands off. Nothing here is personalized: resume and due summaries belong to Practice (/practice/), and the exam board belongs to Exams. Only the Practice call to action changes with local state, as ROUTES.md allows.

Calls I made here

  • One dominant action, both tasks findable. The white button is Practice — “Start practicing” on a first visit, “Continue studying” once local state exists; the outline button is the announcement checker. Neither selects an exam for you.
  • No pill is current. The wordmark is the Home link and carries the current-page state; Practice, Library and Exams stay at rest. There is no exam chip here or anywhere else — no exam is stored, so the header has nothing to name.
  • Overview, not a second Exams page. The cycle appears as one notice plus a hairline date list linking to Exams; the timeline cards and the exam board are not repeated. The three subject areas are the spine of the page, each pointing at the catalog that teaches it.
  • Trust as a fact table. Who runs it, what it costs, where your data lives, where the facts come from, what it is not — five hairline rows, each with its link, instead of prose cards.

What the test covers

Three areas, named by the state test guide for this series and repeated in the two Nassau announcements read on August 25, 2026. Neither the number of questions nor the split between areas is published.

  1. 1

    Cleaning Tools and Their Uses

    Recognizing cleaning tools from a drawing or a description, and matching each one to the job it is meant for.

    Tool atlas Procedures
  2. 2

    Tools Used for Minor Maintenance and Repair

    Hand tools for small repairs, choosing the right one for the task, and knowing when a tool has become unsafe to use.

    Repair lab
  3. 3

    Health and Safety Issues in Custodial Work

    Safe work practices, chemicals and protective equipment, and spotting what is wrong in a workplace scene.

    Hazard scenes

Where this comes from: New York State Department of Civil Service, Test Guide for the Entry-Level Custodians and Janitors Series, and the subjects section of each Nassau announcement, read August 25, 2026.
