Review queue — what is due, and why

The page Study’s “Start review” lands on. It is the same structure as Study one level down: the dark header says how much is due and starts the run, the figures are the learner’s own, and the queue itself is a hairline list scoped by a row of tabs. Reasons are authored and grouped, never inferred; a withdrawn item is held out with its own notice rather than swapped for a newer version of itself.

Calls I made here

  • Study stays the current pill. ROUTES.md places Review in the Study lane, so the pill carries the location and there is no breadcrumb. The link back is the pill itself.
  • The scope tabs scope the run. All, Missed, Flagged and Corrected are the Tabs row above the list — the same row the study hub uses for Recent sessions, counts as plain figures — and each renames the start button, so “Review the 2 you flagged” is one tap. The order inside a scope is the review schedule’s, oldest first.
  • Empty is success. Nothing due reads as a plain statement with the next due date and a session to build instead. No red, no exclamation, and the heading takes focus only after a finished run.
  • A failed read offers a rebuild; history stays. The recovery notice replaces the header band and the due list, but the history section still renders because it is read from the intact attempt events. Export comes before rebuild.
  • Quarantine, not substitution. The withdrawn item gets its own notice below the list, with the attempt and the correction one link away and the version ids under Technical details.

How review works

The short version. Each row links to the full page.

What gets queued
Questions you missed, questions you flagged, and questions corrected after you answered them. Nothing is inferred from how long you took or how sure you seemed.
Reasons
When it comes back
On the review schedule published with the pack, which spaces an item further out each time you answer it as keyed. Flagged items come back until you clear the flag.
Schedule
If a question changes
A correction never edits your result. The item comes back once, marked Superseded, showing the version you saw beside the version that replaced it. A withdrawn item is held out, not swapped.
Corrections
What it is not
Not a score and not a prediction. Clearing the queue means you have seen your own misses again, nothing more.
Scoring
